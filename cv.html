Curriculum Vitae

Teaching, animation & exhibitions

Graduated in Fine Arts (Sculpture) in 1978 from the Bulgarian National Academy of Fine Arts, Academy of Art Nicolás Pavlovich, Sofia. Following several years at Boyana Studios as an animator, he worked as an art director and animator for commercial campaigns in Harare, Zimbabwe (1989–2000). Since 2001 he has been based within the tertiary academic world, teaching primarily Life Drawing, Animation Design and Visual Communication Foundation — in Bogotá from 2001–2010, and in Johannesburg from 2011. His fine art work is held in private collections in Canada, the United States, Spain, Denmark, Sweden, Bulgaria, Colombia, South Africa and Zimbabwe. He is currently based in Sofia, Bulgaria.
